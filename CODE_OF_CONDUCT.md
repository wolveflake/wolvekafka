# Code of conduct

WolveKafka aims to be a respectful, technically rigorous community where
people can contribute regardless of experience or background. This policy
applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone is representing the
project elsewhere.

Be constructive and specific. Assume good intent, explain tradeoffs, credit
others' work, respect different experiences, and make room for newcomers.
Disagreement about code and design is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory or demeaning
language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, publishing someone
else's private information without permission, sustained disruption, and
retaliation for a good-faith report.

If you experience or witness a problem, email
[13@wolveflake.io](mailto:13@wolveflake.io) with the subject **WolveKafka
conduct report**. Include relevant links and context, but do not
send sensitive personal information that is not needed to investigate. Do not
open a public issue to identify a person accused of misconduct.

Wolveflake LLC maintainers will review reports as confidentially as practical,
seek context from the people involved, and decide on a proportionate response.
Possible actions include a reminder, warning, removal of content, a temporary
participation restriction, or a permanent ban. We will avoid disclosing a
reporter's identity without consent except when required by law or necessary
to protect someone's safety. Anyone affected may request reconsideration
through the same private contact.

Maintainers are also subject to this policy. If a maintainer is involved in a
report, a different maintainer should handle it where possible. This is a
community moderation policy, not a promise of legal confidentiality or a
guaranteed response time.
